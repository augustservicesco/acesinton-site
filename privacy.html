Sinton Ace Hardware (105 W Sinton St, Sinton, TX 78387) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you contact us through our website, our Facebook and Instagram ads and forms, or in the store, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what you give us: typically your name, phone number, email address, and details about what you need (for example, screen sizes or an account request).

To answer your request — call you back, send a quote, or follow up on an order or account. We do not use it for anything else without asking you.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except the service providers that deliver it to us (such as Meta, for ad forms, and our email provider). We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to handle your request and keep normal business records. Ask us and we will delete it.
