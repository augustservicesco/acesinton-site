Good to know

Questions We Get a Lot

What are your hours?

We're open six days a week — Monday through Friday 7:30 am to 5:00 pm, and Saturday 8:00 am to 12:00 pm. Saturday morning is the one to catch us on for weekend projects. Closed Sunday.

Do you offer commercial accounts?

Yes — for contractors, ranches, businesses and public agencies. Terms are net 10 with statements on the first of the month, and anyone on your crew can charge at the counter. Request an account or ask at the register.

Can I order online and pick it up in Sinton?

Yes, and it's the easy way to do it. Order on our store page at acehardware.com and we'll have it waiting at the counter — same retail pricing as the website, and nothing to wait on in the mail.

Do you service STIHL and ECHO equipment I bought somewhere else?

Bring it in either way. We take in STIHL, ECHO, EGO and most other small-engine equipment no matter where it came from, and every repair is done right here at hourly rates — your saw or trimmer never leaves Sinton. Manufacturer warranty claims go through the maker's own service network.

How do I pay, and can you hold something for me?

Call us and we'll pull what you need and set it aside with your name on it. Pay at the counter when you come by — cash, credit, debit or an Ace gift card. We also sell gift cards if you're shopping for somebody else.

What's your return policy?

Hang on to your receipt and we'll take care of the return or exchange at the counter.

What are your holiday hours?

Regular hours all season with four exceptions — closed Thanksgiving Day, November 26; open 7:30 am to 12:00 pm Christmas Eve, December 24; closed Christmas Day, December 25; and closed New Year's Day, January 1. The day after Thanksgiving we're open as usual.

Do you sell propane or lumber?

Those two we leave to others. What we do keep on the shelf is everything around the job — fasteners, tools, paint, plumbing and electrical parts, lawn and garden, and grills from Traeger, Weber and Blackstone.
